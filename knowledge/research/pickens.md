## Pickens

**Location**
- City and county seat of Pickens County (formerly called Pickens Courthouse). Source: [Wikipedia, Pickens, SC](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pickens,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch).
- About 18 miles from downtown Greenville (straight line, plus or minus a mile). Source: playbook.md service-area table (Nov 11, 2026).
- Hagood Mill: gristmill built by James Hagood in 1845, about 3 miles north of downtown Pickens on US 178 (NRHP record says 3.5 miles northwest). Listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1972; donated to Pickens County in 1973. Sources: [SC Dept. of Archives and History, Hagood Mill](https://schpr.sc.gov/index.php/Detail/properties/S10817739010); [Wikipedia, Hagood Mill](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Hagood_Mill) (via WebSearch).
- Table Rock State Park: state park in Pickens County, SC, listed as a landmark on the Pickens page. Source: [Google Maps listing](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZVWdQ4RqAxk49ehA8) (supplied by owner, 2026-11-19, with the welcome-kiosk photo). Distance from town not verified, so none is stated in copy.
- Main roads added to the Pickens page: US 123 (Calhoun Memorial Highway) and SC 11 (Cherokee Foothills Scenic Highway). Sources: [Pickens County mitigation plan](https://cms5.revize.com/revize/pickenscountysc/document_center/I%20WAnt%20To/Document%20&%20Forms/Emergency%20Management/0001%20MITIGATION%20PLAN.pdf) lists SC 11 and US 123 among county routes and describes US 123 as running east-west across the southern county from Greenville to Easley and Clemson; [fixcountyroads.com](https://fixcountyroads.com/pickens-county-roads/) lists SC 11 and US 123 as SCDOT-maintained numbered highways (both via WebSearch/Firecrawl excerpts, 2026-11-19). Lane counts and traffic volumes were not verified, so the page does not describe them as "major" or "big".
- Landmarks added to the Pickens page (names only, no distances or dates): Keowee-Toxaway State Park, Pickens County Museum of Art & History, and Hagood-Mauldin House. Sources: [City of Pickens, Our City](https://cityofpickens.com/our-city/) lists all three (Keowee-Toxaway linked to southcarolinaparks.com/keowee-toxaway; Hagood-Mauldin linked to pickenscountyhistoricalsociety.com; museum linked to co.pickens.sc.us museums page); [Pickens County parks and tourism page](https://www.co.pickens.sc.us/departments/parks,_recreation,___tourism/to_do/day_in_pickens.php) names Keowee-Toxaway State Park (via Firecrawl search excerpts, 2026-11-19). Addresses, hours, and distances were not verified.
