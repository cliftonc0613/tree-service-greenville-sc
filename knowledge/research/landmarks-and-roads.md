# Landmarks, main roads, and response areas for city pages

Retrieved 2026-11-16 with WebSearch (Firecrawl out of credits). WebSearch returns summaries, so check each item against its source before launch.

**Response areas are drafts.** Each one lists the town plus its published nearby towns. The business profile has no service-radius field, so the owner must confirm each list before it goes live.

## Simpsonville
- Landmarks: Heritage Park, CCNB Amphitheatre at Heritage Park, replica miniature steam train. Source: [City of Simpsonville, Parks & Facilities](https://www.simpsonville.com/?p=2302) (via WebFetch).
- Roads: I-385 (Fairview Road interchange, Exit 27), SC 14, Fairview Road, SC 417. Sources: [AARoads I-385 North](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/i-385-north-greenville-sc); [AARoads SC 14](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/sc-014) (via WebSearch).

## Taylors
- Landmarks: Taylors Mill (former Southern Bleachery, National Register), Enoree River. Source: [SC Dept. of Archives and History](https://schpr.sc.gov/index.php/Detail/objects/S10817723074) (via WebSearch).
- Roads: US 29 (Wade Hampton Blvd). Source: [AARoads US 29](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/us-029-sc) (via WebSearch).

## Easley
- Landmarks: Nalley Brown Nature Park (38 acres, 380 Adger Road), Saluda River. Source: aggregator and local listings (via WebSearch). Lower confidence; confirm address with Easley Parks.
- Roads: US 123, SC 153, SC 124 (Old Easley Hwy). Sources: [AARoads US 123 North](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/us-123-north-sc); [Wikipedia SC 153](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Carolina_Highway_153) (via WebSearch).

## Greer
- Landmarks: Greenville-Spartanburg International Airport (GSP); BMW plant at 1400 Highway 101 S. Sources: [City of Greer, Doing Business Here](https://cityofgreer.org/o/ed/page/doing-business-here); [BMW Group Plant Spartanburg](https://bmwgroup-werke.com/spartanburg/en/contact.html) (via WebSearch).
- Roads: US 29, SC 101 (BMW address). Source: BMW plant page, above.

## Mauldin
- Landmarks: Mauldin Cultural Center (WPA, 1935 to 1937); W. Jack Greer Branch library at 800 W Butler Road; Gosnell Cabin historical marker. Sources: [Greenville County Library, Mauldin Branch](https://www.greenvillelibrary.org/locations/mauldin); [Living New Deal, Mauldin Cultural Center](https://livingnewdeal.org/projects/mauldin-cultural-center-mauldin-sc/) (via WebSearch).
- Roads: I-385, US 276 (Main Street), Butler Road. Sources: [AARoads I-385 South](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/i-385-south-greenville-sc); see mauldin.md.

## Fountain Inn
- Landmarks: Commerce Park with outdoor amphitheater and farmers market; Ellison Flour Mill (restored); Cedar Falls Park. Source: [SC Picture Project, Cedar Falls Park](https://www.scpictureproject.org/greenville-county/cedar-falls-park.html) and the Fountain Inn search results (via WebSearch). Single search; confirm the flour mill and park details with the city.
- Roads: I-385, SC 14, SC 418. See fountain-inn.md.

## Travelers Rest
- Landmarks: Swamp Rabbit Trail, northern end in town. Source: [TrailLink, Prisma Health Swamp Rabbit Trail](https://www.traillink.com/trail/prisma-health-swamp-rabbit-trail) (via WebSearch).
- Roads: US 25, US 276 (Main Street). See travelers-rest.md.

## Five Forks
- Landmarks: Five Forks Library (Greenville County Library System); Five Forks Place shopping center on Woodruff Road. Sources: search results on the Five Forks area, including [Greenville County, Five Forks Area Plan](https://www.greenvillecounty.org/apps/LongRangePlanning/uploads/FiveForksAreaPlanOrdinance5314.pdf) (via WebSearch).
- Roads: Woodruff Road, SC 14. See five-forks.md.

## Powdersville
- Landmarks: Dolly Cooper Park on the Saluda River; Bethesda Church, founded 1833. Sources: [Anderson County, Dolly Cooper Park](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/dolly-cooper-park-improvements-to-include-accessible-picnic-shelter-and-expanded-parking-lot/); Bethesda date from a local listing (via WebSearch). Bethesda date is single-source.
- Roads: SC 153. Source: [Wikipedia, Powdersville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powdersville,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch).

## Berea
- Landmarks: Sarah Dobey Jones Berea Branch library on N Highway 25 Bypass; Old Berea School site on White Horse Road. Sources: [Greenville County Library, Berea Branch](https://www.greenvillelibrary.org/locations/berea); [SC Picture Project, Old Berea School](https://www.scpictureproject.org/greenville-county/old-berea-school.html) (via WebSearch).
- Roads: US 25 Bypass, White Horse Road. See berea.md.

Sources: [NPS, Piedmont Manufacturing Company Number One](https://home.nps.gov/subjects/nationalhistoriclandmarks/piedmont-manufacturing-company-number-one.htm); [Wikipedia, Piedmont, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedmont,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch).
- Roads: none verified. Roads card is omitted.

## Pickens
- Landmarks: Hagood Mill on US 178 (National Register, 1972). Source: [SC Dept. of Archives and History, Hagood Mill](https://schpr.sc.gov/index.php/Detail/properties/S10817739010) (via WebSearch).
- Roads: US 178. See pickens.md.

- Roads: US 221, SC 101, SC 146 (Main Street). Sources: [AARoads wiki, SC 146](https://wiki.aaroads.com/wiki/South_Carolina_Highway_146); [CorcoHighways route log](https://corcohighways.org/?p=4229) (via WebSearch).
