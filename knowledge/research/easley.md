**Location**
- Easley is in Pickens County.
- US 123 runs from Easley toward Greenville and enters Greenville County at the Saluda River, so the river marks the county line on that route. Sources: [AARoads US 123 North](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/us-123-north-sc); [Wikipedia, US 123](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/US_123) (via WebSearch).
