## Easley

**Demand (playbook.md, DataForSEO, Nov 11, 2026):** "tree service easley sc" 70/month, "tree removal easley sc" 70/month, stump grinding 10. Balanced lead.

**Location**
- Easley is in Pickens County.
- US 123 runs from Easley toward Greenville and enters Greenville County at the Saluda River, so the river marks the county line on that route. Sources: [AARoads US 123 North](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/us-123-north-sc); [Wikipedia, US 123](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/US_123) (via WebSearch). Sources date from 2008 to 2016; confirm the current alignment with SCDOT.
- SC 153 has an interchange with US 123 in Pickens County. Source: [Wikipedia, SC 153](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Carolina_Highway_153) (via WebSearch). Not used in copy.
