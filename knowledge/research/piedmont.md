## Piedmont

**Location**
- Census-designated place (unincorporated) along the Saluda River in Anderson AND Greenville counties. 2020 Census population 5,411. Source: [Wikipedia, Piedmont, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedmont,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch).
- About 11 miles from downtown Greenville (straight line, plus or minus a mile). Source: playbook.md service-area table (Nov 11, 2026).
- History: the site was known as "Big Shoals of the Saluda", then Garrison Shoals after a grist mill around 1850. Henry Pinckney Hammett founded the Piedmont Manufacturing Company in 1873 and built a textile mill and village at the shoals. Piedmont Number One became a National Historic Landmark in 1978 and burned in 1983. Sources: Wikipedia, above; [NPS, Piedmont Manufacturing Company Number One](https://home.nps.gov/subjects/nationalhistoriclandmarks/piedmont-manufacturing-company-number-one.htm) (via WebSearch).

### Trees, rules, and the river (added 2026-11-16)
Retrieved with WebSearch and WebFetch (Firecrawl out of credits).
- Piedmont Riverfront Park: Anderson County's 21-acre ecotourism park on the Saluda River in Piedmont, reached by an access road off River Road. Phase One has an ADA kayak launch (part of the 48-Mile Saluda River Blueway), a half-mile marked nature trail built with the Upstate Greenways and Trails Alliance, and a picnic shelter. The page describes dramatic river bluffs and protected wetlands, at the confluence of the Saluda River and Big Brushy Creek. "Tree plantings" is listed once among project elements with no detail. Funding $1,512,000 (including a $500,000 federal Land and Water Conservation Fund grant). Source: [Anderson County, Piedmont Riverfront Park opens on the Saluda River](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/anderson-countys-piedmont-riverfront-park-opens-on-the-saluda-river/) (via WebFetch). The announcement gives an unveiling date of May 8, 2026, and no street address. The copy gives no date or address.
- County rules: Greenville County's tree preservation article, in a 2023 public review draft, applies to new development in unincorporated areas needing a grading permit (see service-tree-removal.md). **Anderson County's residential tree rules were not found**: the search returned only a zoning-site page on tree canopy retention priority areas and other counties' ordinances. The copy tells readers to check with their county and makes no Anderson County permit claim.
