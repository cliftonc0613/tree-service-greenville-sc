## ISA Certified Arborist
- A credential from the International Society of Arboriculture. Earned by passing a written exam (200 multiple-choice questions, 3.5 hours) and showing documented field experience. Renewal needs continuing education (30 CEUs every 3 years). Sources: [ISA, credential info](https://wwv.isa-arbor.com/certification); [ISA application](https://www.isa-arbor.com/portals/0/assets/pdf/certification-applications/cert-application-certified-arborist.pdf) (via WebSearch).

## Tree risk assessment levels
- ISA Best Management Practices: Tree Risk Assessment describes three levels: Level 1 limited visual (quick check from one spot, often used to screen), Level 2 basic (ground-based 360-degree inspection of the tree and site, the level most arborists use for private clients), Level 3 advanced (detailed study of specific factors, sometimes special equipment, needs client approval). TRAQ covers Levels 1 and 2. Sources: [UF/IFAS EDIS tree risk assessment](https://ojs.test.flvc.org/edis/article/download/131937/135518/237741); [Classic City Arborists](https://www.classiccityarborists.com/news-and-blog/tree-risk-assessments2502529) (via WebSearch).
