## Wade Hampton

**Location**
- Census-designated place (unincorporated) in central Greenville County, named for Civil War general and South Carolina governor Wade Hampton. The city of Greenville is on its southwest edge and Taylors is to the northeast. 2020 population 21,482 (Wikipedia). Source: [Wikipedia, Wade Hampton, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wade_Hampton,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch).
- Wade Hampton Boulevard (US 29) is a commercial arterial running northeast through the Wade Hampton and Taylors areas. Sources: [Wikipedia, US 29 in SC](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_29_(South_Carolina)); [AARoads US 29](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/us-029-sc) (via WebSearch, see taylors.md).
