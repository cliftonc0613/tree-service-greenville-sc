## Travelers Rest

**Location**
- City in Greenville County, about 10 miles north of Greenville; the northernmost city in the county. 2020 Census population 7,788. Source: [Wikipedia, Travelers Rest, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travelers_Rest,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch).
- US 25 passes along the east side of the city heading north toward the Blue Ridge and Hendersonville, NC. US 276 runs from downtown Greenville and becomes Travelers Rest's Main Street. Sources: Wikipedia, above; [AARoads US 25](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/us-025-sc) (via WebSearch).
- The Prisma Health Swamp Rabbit Trail runs between Greenville and Travelers Rest; its northern end is in Travelers Rest (TrailLink lists Tate Rd south of White Horse Rd). Sources: [TrailLink](https://www.traillink.com/trail/prisma-health-swamp-rabbit-trail); [VisitGreenvilleSC](https://www.visitgreenvillesc.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/swamp-rabbit-trail/) (via WebSearch).

### History of Travelers Rest (added 2026-11-16)
- Name: from its days as a stopover for travelers. One account says William Bishop ran an inn where drovers slept indoors while their animals were penned outside; another says it was a stopping point for cattle drovers from Kentucky, North Carolina, and Tennessee. A Greenville County Historical Society paper (scanned, 1960s era) says stagecoaches on the Asheville to Augusta or Columbia route also paused here. Sources: [Travelers Rest, "But What About that Name?"](https://travelersrestsc.com/?p=6761); [Greenville County Historical Society paper](https://d8e7jbdw4fu0e.cloudfront.net/17224/be86ffa0-2e13-11f0-8afd-392fc7de7a74-vUXupf8.full.pdf); [SC Encyclopedia](https://scencyclopedia.org/sce/?p=11459) (via WebSearch). The copy says only "a stopover for travelers and cattle drovers".
- Spelling: "Travellers Rest" until the mid-1940s, when postmaster William F. Griffin officially changed it (same historical society paper). Single document; confirm with the Travelers Rest Historical Society.
- Not used: the 1808 founding date (single source) and any incorporation date (not found). A Toccoa, Georgia "Travelers Rest" inn appeared in results and is a different place.

### History Museum of Travelers Rest (added 2026-11-16)
- 1926 building, built at 8 S. Main Street by W.H. Vest as a grocery store. Donated to the Travelers Rest Historical Society in 2010 on condition it be moved; it now stands at 3 Edwards Street on land donated by the Bob Roe family, faced with bricks from the former Travelers Rest High School (built 1956, razed 2011). Sources: [Upcountry SC listing](https://upcountrysc.com/listing-details/travelers-rest/history-museum-of-travelers-rest/61075555/); [Travelers Rest, museum page](https://travelersrestsc.com/?p=797) (via WebSearch).
