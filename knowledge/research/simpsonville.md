# Service area research: Simpsonville, SC

Retrieved 2026-11-16 with WebSearch (Firecrawl was out of credits; WebSearch results are summaries, so treat exact figures as needing a page check before publishing).

## Simpsonville

**Demand (from playbook.md, DataForSEO, Nov 11, 2026):** "tree removal simpsonville sc" 210/month, "tree service" 50/month, trimming 10, stump grinding 10. Local organic results are mostly Facebook pages, Reddit threads, Yelp, and small tree companies (Econo Tree Man, Tony Bridges). Map pack not checked live.

**Location**
- Southeastern Greenville County. Mauldin is to the northwest; Fountain Inn is to the southeast. Source: [Wikipedia, Simpsonville, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simpsonville,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch).
- Interstate 385 connects Simpsonville north to Greenville. One source describes a six-ramp interchange with Fairview Road. Source: [AARoads, I-385 North](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/i-385-north-greenville-sc) (via WebSearch; guide dated, check SCDOT for current interchange detail).
- Fairview Road runs south from SC 14 (SE Main Street) through commercial areas. Source: AARoads SC 14 guide, [aaroads.com/guides/sc-014](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/sc-014) (via WebSearch).
- Heritage Park: roughly 90-acre park in southern Simpsonville, with athletic fields, playgrounds, a miniature train, and the CCNB Amphitheatre. Source: Wikipedia mirror summaries (via WebSearch). Amphitheater capacity conflicts (10,000 vs 15,000), so the copy does not state a capacity.

**Not confirmed / do not use in copy**
- "Five Forks area on its north side" (playbook claim). SC 14 runs through Five Forks, a Greenville suburb near Mauldin, but no source places it on Simpsonville's north side. Dropped.
- Population: sources conflict. 2010 Census 18,238; 2020 Census figures range from 23,354 to 25,272; 2025 estimate 27,108 (per playbook, citypopulation.de 28,459). Not used in copy because the city template does not render population.
- Incorporation date 1901 and mayor name: single-source, not used.
- Official City of Simpsonville website: not found in search results.
