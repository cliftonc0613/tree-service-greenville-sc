## Mauldin

**Location**
- Mauldin is on Interstate 385 between Greenville and Simpsonville. Butler Road runs from US 276 (Main Street) at the Mauldin city center to I-385 Exit 34, a diamond interchange. Sources: [AARoads I-385 South](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/i-385-south-greenville-sc); [Greenville County Schools, Mauldin Middle directions](https://greenville.k12.sc.us/mauldinm/map) (via WebSearch).
- Nearby exits: Exit 33 Bridges Road, Exit 35 SC 146 (Woodruff Road). Source: AARoads, above. Not used in copy.
- The East Butler Road bridge over I-385 reopened after rehabilitation work. Source: [FOX Carolina, Aug 3, 2026](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/08/03/bridge-over-i-385-mauldin-reopens-after-rehabilitation-work/) (via WebSearch). Not used in copy.
