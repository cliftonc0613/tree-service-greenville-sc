**Location**
- Wade Hampton Boulevard is US 29, a commercial arterial running northeast through the Wade Hampton and Taylors areas. Sources: [Wikipedia, US 29 in SC](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_29_(South_Carolina)); [AARoads US 29](https://www.aaroads.com/guides/us-029-sc) (via WebSearch).
- US 29 (Wade Hampton Blvd) crosses the Enoree River in Greenville County; SCDOT lists a bridge rehabilitation project. Source: [SCDOT project portal](https://projectportal.scdot.org/US29bridgeoverEnoreeRiver) (via WebSearch). Exact bridge location relative to Taylors not confirmed, so copy says only "Enoree River nearby".
- Taylors Mill: the former Southern Bleachery and Print Works, a textile finishing plant opened in the 1920s (sources say 1923-24 or 1924) and listed on the National Register of Historic Places. It sits near downtown Taylors and is now a mixed-use campus. Sources: [SC Dept. of Archives and History](https://schpr.sc.gov/index.php/Detail/objects/S10817723074); [Wikipedia, Southern Bleachery and Print Works](https://www.wikipedia.com/wiki/Southern_Bleachery_and_Print_Works) (via WebSearch).
