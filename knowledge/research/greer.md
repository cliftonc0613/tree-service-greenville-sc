**Location**
- Greer is a city in Greenville and Spartanburg counties, about 12 miles northeast of Greenville and 17 miles west of Spartanburg, both via US 29. 2020 Census population 35,308. Source: [Wikipedia, Greer, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greer,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch, mirror copies).
- Greenville-Spartanburg International Airport (GSP) is next to the city, just outside the city limits. Source: [City of Greer, Doing Business Here](https://cityofgreer.org/o/ed/page/doing-business-here) (via WebSearch).
- BMW Manufacturing's plant is at 1400 Highway 101 S, Greer, SC 29651. Source: [BMW Group Plant Spartanburg contact page](https://bmwgroup-werke.com/spartanburg/en/contact.html) (via WebSearch).

### Downtown Greer (added 2026-11-16)
- Greer Depot, 311 Trade Street: built 1913 by the Piedmont & Northern Railroad as a passenger station and freight warehouse; National Register listed. Sources: [SC Dept. of Archives and History, Greer Depot](https://schpr.sc.gov/index.php/Detail/properties/S10817723035); [Greer Depot, NPS](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/100004447) (via WebSearch).
- Greer Downtown Historic District: National Register listed 1997, along Trade, East Poinsett, Randall, Victoria, and North Main streets. Source: [Wikipedia, Greer Downtown Historic District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greer_Downtown_Historic_District) (via WebSearch). A 2019 boundary amendment is mentioned by one source; the copy does not name building counts.
- Greer Heritage Museum: in the former Greer Post Office, built 1935 by the WPA. Source: [Greer Heritage Museum listing](https://greerheritage.com/omeka-s/s/museum/media/303) (via WebSearch). Address conflicts between sources (106 Main vs 106 S Main), so the copy gives no street number.
- Trade Street: converted to a flexible shared street that can close to traffic for events. Source: [Wikipedia, Greer Downtown Historic District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greer_Downtown_Historic_District) (via WebSearch).
