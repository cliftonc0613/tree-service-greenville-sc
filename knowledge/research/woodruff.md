## Woodruff

**Location**
- City in the southwestern part of Spartanburg County, in the Upstate. 2020 Census population 4,333 (playbook lists 6,026; not used). Source: [Wikipedia, Woodruff, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodruff,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch).
- About 22 miles from downtown Greenville (straight line, plus or minus a mile). Source: playbook.md service-area table. A real estate page says about 25 miles; copy uses the playbook figure with "about".
- US 221, SC 101, and SC 146 meet in Woodruff and run together through downtown as Main Street. Sources: [AARoads wiki, SC 146](https://wiki.aaroads.com/wiki/South_Carolina_Highway_146); [Wikipedia, US 221 in SC](https://www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_221_in_South_Carolina); [CorcoHighways route log](https://corcohighways.org/?p=4229) (via WebSearch).
- SC 146 is Woodruff Road in Greenville County (see simpsonville.md and mauldin.md: I-385 Exit 35 is SC 146 / Woodruff Road).
