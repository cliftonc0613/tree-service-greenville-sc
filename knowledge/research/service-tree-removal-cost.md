## Homeowners insurance and tree removal
- Coverage usually applies when a covered event (wind, hail, lightning, fire) brings a tree down onto a covered structure such as the house, garage, fence, or shed. Debris removal is often capped, with limits per tree and per incident that vary by policy. Preventive removal of a healthy tree is generally not covered. Vehicles are usually handled by auto insurance. Sources: [GEICO](https://www.geico.com/information/aboutinsurance/homeowners/does-homeowners-insurance-cover-tree-removal/); [Policygenius](https://policygenius.com/homeowners-insurance/does-homeowners-insurance-cover-tree-removal); [PropertyCasualty360, debris removal coverage](https://www.propertycasualty360.com/amp/2017/09/13/when-a-tree-falls-is-there-debris-removal-coverage) (via WebSearch). Insurer and broker sources, not the Insurance Information Institute or the SC Department of Insurance. Copy stays general and states no dollar limits.
