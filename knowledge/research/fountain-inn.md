## Fountain Inn

**Location**
- Fountain Inn is a city in Greenville and Laurens counties. 2020 Census population 10,416; Census QuickFacts estimate 13,027 for July 1, 2023. Sources: [Wikipedia, Fountain Inn, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountain_Inn,_South_Carolina); [Census QuickFacts](https://census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/fountaininncitysouthcarolina/PST045223) (via WebSearch).
- Southeast of Simpsonville. Source: [Wikipedia, Simpsonville, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simpsonville,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch; see simpsonville.md).
- I-385 serves Fountain Inn at Exit 23 (SC 418, toward Fork Shoals and Fountain Inn). Source: [Cross Country Roads I-385 southbound photo log](https://crosscountryroads.com/photos/south-carolina/sc-i385sb) (via WebSearch). Photo log, so confirm the exit number with SCDOT.
- SC 14 carries the former US 276 alignment through Fountain Inn toward Laurens. US 276 itself now stays in Greenville County and does not pass through Fountain Inn. Source: [AARoads wiki, US 276](https://wiki.aaroads.com/wiki/U.S._Route_276) (via WebSearch).

### Ellison Flour Mill (added 2026-11-16)
- Built 1940 to 1941 by F.G. Ellison; the town's only flour mill, later the Fountain Inn Milling Company. Stopped milling in the 1970s. National Register listed. Sources: [SC Dept. of Archives and History, Ellison Flour Mill](https://schpr.sc.gov/index.php/Detail/objects/S10817723094); [Preserve SC, 2025 honor](https://www.preservesc.org/2025-sc-historic-preservation-aw/ellison-flour-mill) (via WebSearch).
- Restored as a brewery and food hall; Main Street SC describes it as an anchor to the downtown district. Source: [Main Street SC](https://www.masc.sc/node/6561) (via WebSearch).
- Not used: the largest outdoor screen claim (single source); the NRHP build date range (1940 to 1972) conflicts with the 1940 to 1941 construction date, so the copy uses 1940 and 1941.

### Fountain Inn Museum (added 2026-11-16)
- Fountain Inn Museum at 102 Depot Street, Fountain Inn; exhibits on the town's history, plus a visitor center sponsored by the Chamber of Commerce. Sources: [Fountain Inn Heritage Museum & Farmers Market (DP3 Architects)](https://dp3architects.com/?p=87); [Fountain Inn Museum listing](https://fountain-inn-sc.yiftee.com/business/fountain-inn-museum-534716) (via WebSearch).
- Rotating exhibits, including a summer exhibit in 2026. Source: [Who's On the Move](https://whosonthemove.com/?p=492931) (via WebSearch).
