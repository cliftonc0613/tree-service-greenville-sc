## Fountain Inn

**Location**
- Fountain Inn is a city in Greenville and Laurens counties. 2020 Census population 10,416; Census QuickFacts estimate 13,027 for July 1, 2023. Sources: [Wikipedia, Fountain Inn, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountain_Inn,_South_Carolina); [Census QuickFacts](https://census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/fountaininncitysouthcarolina/PST045223) (via WebSearch).
- Southeast of Simpsonville. Source: [Wikipedia, Simpsonville, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simpsonville,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch; see simpsonville.md).
- I-385 serves Fountain Inn at Exit 23 (SC 418, toward Fork Shoals and Fountain Inn). Source: [Cross Country Roads I-385 southbound photo log](https://crosscountryroads.com/photos/south-carolina/sc-i385sb) (via WebSearch). Photo log, so confirm the exit number with SCDOT.
- SC 14 carries the former US 276 alignment through Fountain Inn toward Laurens. US 276 itself now stays in Greenville County and does not pass through Fountain Inn. Source: [AARoads wiki, US 276](https://wiki.aaroads.com/wiki/U.S._Route_276) (via WebSearch).
