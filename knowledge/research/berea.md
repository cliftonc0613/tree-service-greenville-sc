### Swamp Rabbit Trail (added 2026-11-16)
- The Swamp Rabbit Trail runs between Greenville and Travelers Rest, and a trail itinerary places a station in Berea halfway between the two, with a pocket park that has an information kiosk, seating, and a water refill station. Source: Roadtrippers and Greenville tourism itineraries (via WebSearch); single-type source, so confirm the station details with Greenville County Parks, Recreation and Tourism. The copy says only "a trail station in Berea".
- The Google Maps link supplied for the photo opens a general Swamp Rabbit Trail listing near downtown Greenville, not a Berea-specific point.

### Trees, county rules, and nearby woods (added 2026-11-16)
Retrieved with WebSearch (Firecrawl out of credits).
- Berea is unincorporated, so Greenville County's rules apply directly; the city code does not reach it. Source: a third-party rules site (lead only, not used as the authority). The county's tree preservation article, in a public review draft dated August 2023, applies to new development in unincorporated areas that need a grading permit. Source: [Greenville County tree preservation draft](https://www.greenvillecounty.org/planning/pdf/07.Tree%20Preservation.Public%20Review%20Draft%2008-11-23.pdf) (via WebSearch). Adoption of the final rule is not confirmed; the copy says "as drafted".
- Canopy: a county tree canopy study, reported in local news, names Berea, Parker, and Sans Souci among areas with lower canopy coverage than others, and describes plans for stronger tree protection in the county's development code. Source: [AOL, Greenville County releases new tree canopy study](https://www.aol.com/greenville-county-releases-tree-canopy-091201573.html) (via WebSearch; one news report, check the county study itself before quoting numbers).
- Paris Mountain State Park, nearby: mixed hardwoods and pines, about 14 to 15 miles of trails. Sources: travel and trail aggregators (via WebSearch; low confidence on detail). Used only as general tree context, not as a landmark.
