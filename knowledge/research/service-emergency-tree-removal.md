## Downed power lines
- Duke Energy says to stay at least 30 feet back from a downed line, treat every downed line as live even if it is not sparking, and report the hazard to the utility or emergency services. If a line is on a vehicle, stay inside and call 911 unless there is immediate danger such as fire. Source: [Duke Energy, Safety Around Power Lines](https://www.duke-energy.com/resource-hub/residential/for-your-safety/safety-around-power-lines) (via WebSearch).
- Duke Energy serves most of the Greenville area (search summary). Which utility serves an address is on the customer's bill. Copy tells readers to use the utility named on their bill.
- **Correction:** a search result suggested "Greenville Utilities (GUC)" as a separate utility with its own hotline. GUC is Greenville Utilities Commission in Greenville, North Carolina, not South Carolina. Not used. Dominion Energy SC's page also appeared and does not serve Greenville. Not used.
- No phone numbers are used in copy. An older Duke number could not be confirmed as current.

## Insurance and storm claims
- Same sources as the cost page: coverage usually applies when a covered event drops a tree on a covered structure; debris removal is often capped per tree and per incident; vehicles are usually handled by auto insurance. See service-tree-removal-cost.md.
