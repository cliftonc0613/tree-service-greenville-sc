## Five Forks

**Location**
- Census-designated place (unincorporated) in eastern Greenville County, about 11 miles east-southeast of downtown Greenville. 2020 Census population 17,737. Source: [Wikipedia, Five Forks, SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Forks,_South_Carolina) (via WebSearch).
- Simpsonville is about 6.1 miles south and Mauldin about 4.5 miles southwest of Five Forks, so Five Forks is north of Simpsonville and northeast of Mauldin. Source: [Roadside Thoughts, Five Forks](https://roadsidethoughts.com/sc/five-forks-xx-greenville-census.htm) (via WebSearch). Secondary source; directions agree with Wikipedia's "eastern Greenville County".
- The name comes from the meeting point of five major roads. Woodruff Road is an east/west corridor and the area's commercial hub. Source: [Greenville County, Five Forks Area Plan](https://www.greenvillecounty.org/apps/LongRangePlanning/uploads/FiveForksAreaPlanOrdinance5314.pdf) (via WebSearch).

**Note:** The Simpsonville page does not say "Five Forks on its north side". That wording implies the two border each other, which no source states. "North of Simpsonville" on this page is supported by the distance listing.
